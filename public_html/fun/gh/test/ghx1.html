Instructions


Move device until enemy is in target and fire one of the buttons. You can pan left, right, up and down.

To move up to the next level you must destroy all the enemies before the time runs out. There is less time to destroy the enemies as you move up in levels.

There are more points for shooting enemies that are far away or have a bonus sign. The bonus is 500 to 1500 points based on distance. There is an additional 750 bonus points if the enemy with a bonus is the last one destroyed on a level.
